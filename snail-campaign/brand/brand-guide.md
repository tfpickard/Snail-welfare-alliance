## Color Palette

The hero color is Tyrian purple — not the gaudy violet of cheap packaging, but the deep, almost bruised reddish-purple that the ancients prized above gold. Paired with the warmth of aged paper and the precision of iron-gall ink.

| Role | Name | Hex | RGB | Use |
|------|------|-----|-----|-----|
| Hero | Tyrian Purple | `#5C0A3A` | 92, 10, 58 | Primary brand color; headlines, CTA buttons, key strokes |
| Deep | Imperial Dark | `#3A0025` | 58, 0, 37 | Deep shadow tone; dark backgrounds, print fills |
| Accent | Murex Bloom | `#8B1A5C` | 139, 26, 92 | Lighter purple for hover states, highlights |
| Background | Vellum | `#F5F0E8` | 245, 240, 232 | Off-white background; never pure white |
| Light neutral | Bone | `#EDE6D4` | 237, 230, 212 | Secondary backgrounds, card fills |
| Ink | Engraver's Black | `#1A1209` | 26, 18, 9 | All line work; near-black, warm-toned, not pure #000 |
| Shell | Murex Ivory | `#D4C5A0` | 212, 197, 160 | Shell highlights, subtle decorative use |

## Typography

We do not specify exact typefaces for the web build phase (that comes later), but the direction is locked:

### Headline / Display
**Direction:** Serif with presence. Think engraved historical type — a high-contrast serif with sharp, slightly antiquarian character. Not decorative, not distressed. Elegant and authoritative.
- *Reference feel:* Trajan, Cormorant Garamond, EB Garamond, Freight Display
- Weight: Regular to SemiBold only — heavy weights undermine the elegance
- Case: Title case for main headlines; sentence case for body

### Body / UI
**Direction:** A humanist sans-serif for clarity and modern readability.
- *Reference feel:* Inter, DM Sans, Instrument Sans
- Body text: 16–18px / 1.6 line-height minimum

### Accent / Callout
**Direction:** Small caps or tracked caps for stat callouts, navigation, labels. No condensed display type.

### What we avoid
- Script fonts
- Slab serifs (too hearty; wrong register)
- Any typeface that reads as whimsical or childlike

## Visual Style

### Core principle
**Line art. Single weight. Full stop.**

Every illustration and logo is built from clean, single-stroke line work — as if drawn with a fine dip pen, then digitized. The reference is natural-history engraving (think 18th-century botanical illustration, Audubon-adjacent) crossed with the restraint of modern minimalist tattooing.

### Rules
- Single stroke weight throughout each mark (±10% taper allowed at terminations)
- No fills except where color is applied as a flat wash *behind* line work
- No gradients in print assets; subtle linear gradients allowed in digital hero pieces only
- No drop shadows; no bevels; no 3D
- Organic curves encouraged — snails are geometry in motion
- Negative space is compositional, not empty

### What we do not do
- Cartoon proportions or exaggerated features
- Any gore, wound imagery, or graphic harm depiction
- Photorealism
- Clip art aesthetic
- "Cute mascot" energy

### The emotional register
The snail is beautiful, ancient, and alive. It is also being systematically ignored. Our visual language holds both truths: elegance and quiet indictment. A mound of empty cracked shells is more damning than any photograph of a wound.

## Logo Usage

Full documentation is in `brand/logos/` and `brand/wordmarks/`. Summary rules:

1. **Clear space:** Minimum 1× the cap-height of the wordmark on all sides of the lockup
2. **Minimum size:** Primary mark no smaller than 24px / 6mm in any dimension; horizontal lockup no smaller than 120px wide
3. **Approved color combos:**
   - Engraver's Black mark on Vellum or Bone backgrounds
   - White mark on Tyrian Purple or Imperial Dark backgrounds
   - Tyrian Purple mark on Vellum or white
4. **Never:**
   - Stretch or distort
   - Apply drop shadows
   - Use on photographic backgrounds without a solid field behind
   - Combine the mark with competing illustration at same scale
5. **Compact mark:** The SWA monogram (`mark-monogram-swa.svg`) is the compact mark for small applications — favicons, app icons, embroidery, stamp-size uses. Use the full lockup wherever space allows.
6. **Wordmark:** All wordmarks and lockups now read **Snail Welfare Alliance**.

## Voice & Tone

### Character
Intelligent. Wry. Unambiguous. We do not moralise; we present facts that are so absurd they make the moral argument for us. We trust the reader.

### The three gears

**Gear 1 — The case (facts page, homepage):** Clear, precise, unsentimental. Every claim sourced. No inflation. We are building trust; credibility is our most valuable asset.

**Gear 2 — The emotional gear (hero copy, merch):** Vivid and occasionally disarming. The snail is beautiful. The act is unnecessary. We can hold these plainly without flinching. Occasional wry humor earns its place only when it doesn't undercut the gravity.

**Gear 3 — The invite (donate, newsletter, social):** Warm, personal, direct. No guilt. No savior complex. "This is easy to fix and you can help" — that's it.

### Hard rules
- **Never claim sentience as proven.** Always frame as precautionary: "possibly sentient," "may experience pain," "the biology is suggestive."
- **Never attack practitioners by name.** Mohamed Nouira and Ptil Tekhelet are named in our research because they are public figures in public reporting — not as villains. We target the *demand* for lethal natural purple, not the struggling artisan.
- **Always carve out and celebrate Mexico.** The Mixtec non-lethal tradition is proof of concept, not an exception to explain away.
- **No gore.** The imagination and the math do the work.
- **No preachy hedging.** Don't write "while we understand that snail welfare is a complex issue..." — we don't hedge, we state.

### Vocabulary to use
- "unnecessary" / "needless" / "gratuitous"
- "identical molecule" / "same compound" / "synthetic twin"
- "possibly sentient" / "precautionary principle"
- "purple without death" (for the Mexico story)
- "the color of emperors, the cost of everything"

### Vocabulary to avoid
- "murder" / "slaughter" (re: snails — too inflammatory, invites mockery)
- "torture" (unverified; inflammatory)
- "natural is better" (never even as a thing-we-refute; it elevates the claim)
- "just" as a minimizer ("it's just a snail") — don't put this in their mouths

## Campaign & Sub-brand Names

The organization name is locked: **Snail Welfare Alliance** (SWA). The brand operates on three layers:

| Layer | Name | Notes |
|-------|------|-------|
| Parent org | **Snail Welfare Alliance** (SWA) | The institution. All legal, domain, and formal references. |
| Flagship campaign | **"Where Are the Snails?"** | Awareness campaign under SWA. Preserve; do not collapse into the org name. |
| Merch sub-brand | **"Save the Snails"** | Apparel and merch line only. Lives on t-shirt and product copy. |

The "Alliance" framing is deliberate: this is an open coalition across backgrounds — researchers, designers, donors, advocates — not a top-down charity asking for sympathy. Copy should reflect that register where natural.

The remaining open choice is which tagline to run on the homepage. Candidates are in `copy/taglines.md`; the shortlist recommendation is **"The color of emperors. The cost of everything."**
