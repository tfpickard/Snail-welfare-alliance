# Merch / Print Notes

## General

All designs are provided as SVG masters. For print-on-demand, export to PNG at **300 DPI**. The SVG viewBox dimensions are in "design pixels" — use them to calculate your target resolution:

- A 10" print at 300 DPI = 3000px. If the viewBox is 2400px wide, export at **125% scale** to hit 3000px.
- A 12" print at 300 DPI = 3600px.

**Background:** All designs are on transparent background except where a colored background is specified (none of the current designs require one — shirt color is the background). Always export with transparent background from your SVG tool.

**Ink colors:** All current designs are single-color (Engraver's Black `#1A1209`). For dark shirt variants, convert to `#F5F0E8` (Vellum) or pure white before export.

## Print-on-Demand Setup (Printful, Printify, or similar)

1. **File format:** Upload SVG or high-res PNG (300 DPI minimum). Most POD services accept PNG; some accept SVG directly.
2. **Color mode:** Convert to CMYK if the platform requires it. These designs are black-only (or off-white on dark) — no complex color conversion needed.
3. **Bleed:** These designs do not require bleed (they don't go to the edge of the garment).
4. **Mockup colors:** For bone/natural shirts, request "natural," "ivory," or "oatmeal" color options. For dark designs, request "black" or "charcoal."
5. **Minimum order:** Order samples before bulk production — line art can vary by printing method (DTG vs. screen print).

### DTG vs. Screen Print
- **DTG (Direct-to-Garment):** Fine for small runs; handles fine line detail well on light shirts. Quality on dark shirts can vary — request a sample.
- **Screen Print:** Better for bulk/dark shirts. Single-color designs (all of these) are inexpensive to screen print. Minimum runs typically 24–48 units per design.

These designs scale cleanly to:
