## Body

The market for natural Tyrian purple is tiny. A few collectors, a handful of religious institutions, a handful of artists. That's the whole thing. Which means the total amount of effort required to end the lethal practice is also small — if it's organized and targeted.

Here's what your donation funds:

- **Research and documentation** — tracking producers, mapping the supply chain, commissioning analysis of the science
- **Outreach** — reaching the buyers and institutions who make lethal production viable
- **Public awareness** — this website, the campaign materials, the work of making this visible
- **Legal monitoring** — watching for regulatory opportunities as invertebrate welfare law develops

We're a small operation. Every dollar goes directly to campaign work. No overhead theater.
