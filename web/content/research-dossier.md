# Research Dossier — Tyrian Purple & Snail Welfare
*Source of truth for all campaign copy. Do not dilute or inflate claims beyond what is stated here.*

---

## The Hook

Tyrian purple — the legendary "color of emperors" — is still made today by cracking open live predatory sea snails and extracting a gland. The yield: roughly **10,000–12,000 snails per single gram** of dye. The price: approximately **€2,500–$4,000 per gram**. The necessity: **zero** — the exact same molecule (6,6′-dibromoindigo) can be synthesized in a lab or grown by fermentation with no snails at all. People still kill them because they believe the natural version "looks better" — a claim that collapses on inspection, because it is the identical molecule.

---

## The Big Picture

Snail production at large operates with essentially no ethical standards or welfare oversight. Tyrian purple is the vivid, absurd tip of the spear. The broader mission is invertebrate/snail welfare — an analogue to the Shrimp Welfare Project, though we won't necessarily use that framing.

- Spent ~14 years reverse-engineering the Phoenician method
- Buys *Bolinus brandaris* and related murex at La Goulette port

### Israel — Ptil Tekhelet (Kfar Adumim)
- Co-founder: Baruch Sterman
- Largest organized producer
- Processes approximately **400,000 snail glands per year**
- Makes ~20,000 ritual *tzitzit* sets/year; **~30 snails per set**
- 270,000+ sets dyed to date
- Species: *Hexaplex trunculus* (partly sourced as food-industry byproduct)
- **Method: lethal**
- Context: religious use under Jewish law (the *tekhelet* commandment); handle with care and respect — we promote substitution, we do not attack practitioners

### Mexico — Mixtec dyers of Pinotepa de Don Luis, Oaxaca ★ OUR HERO EXAMPLE
- Species: *Purpura pansa*
- **Method: NON-LETHAL** — "milking": the living snail is pressed to release its secretion onto thread/cloth, then returned alive to the rocks
- A ~3,000-year-old tradition; regulated by the Mexican government since 1988
- Approximately 13–15 elderly licensed dyers remain
- Near-total collapse in the early 1980s: a Japanese firm (Imperial Purple Inc.) paid untrained volume harvesters who killed the snails, devastating local populations. Recovery took decades.
- **This is proof that purple does not require death**

### Japan — Masakazu Akiyama (Aya studio) & researcher Takako Terada
- Revived "shellfish purple" in Japan
- Lethal gland extraction
- Akiyama's dyed textiles were presented to the Empress
- **Method: lethal**

**Global annual kill specifically for dye:** No exact figure exists. A defensible order-of-magnitude estimate is **under ~1 million/year** — label this clearly as an estimate when used.

### Scale in context
- Food snails killed annually: **2.9–7.7 billion** (Rethink Priorities; typically boiled alive)
- Shrimp: **trillions/year**
- Dye snails: roughly **a millionth** of the food-snail kill

**The framing we use:** Scale is orthogonal to ethics. A needless act of killing a possibly-sentient animal for a luxury color is wrong on a per-act basis. And *precisely because* the scale is tiny and the alternative is free, this is the easiest needless harm to abolish.

## The Science (state precisely — do NOT overclaim)

### What snails/gastropods demonstrably have
- Nociceptors (pain-receptor neurons)
- An opioid system with a μ-opioid receptor approximately **95% identical** to the human μ-opioid receptor
- Long-term nociceptive sensitization in *Aplysia* (a sea slug) functionally similar to chronic pain

### What is NOT established
- That these specific sea snails consciously *feel* or *suffer* pain — no formal sentience assessment exists for *Bolinus brandaris*, *Hexaplex trunculus*, or *Purpura pansa* specifically

### Comparative anchor (use this)
- Cephalopods (octopus, cuttlefish, squid) are ALSO mollusks and are now **legally recognized as sentient**:
  - UK Animal Welfare (Sentience) Act 2022
  - EU recognition of decapod crustaceans and cephalopods
- Their gastropod cousins are simply **understudied**, not ruled out

### The 2024 New York Declaration on Animal Consciousness
- Signatories include Christof Koch, Anil Seth, David Chalmers
- Affirms a "realistic possibility" of consciousness in many invertebrates
- We cite this as a statement of expert opinion, not proof

### Our stated premise
> Assume a capacity for pain until proven otherwise — because pain is a primal survival mechanism, and the cost of being wrong is borne entirely by the snail.

## "You Can't Tell Them Apart"

Natural and synthetic Tyrian purple are the **same molecule**: 6,6′-dibromoindigo. The Tekhelet tradition itself holds that the dye is visually indistinguishable from indigo ("only God can tell them apart"). Any perceived difference in natural vs. synthetic dye baths derives from impurities and bath composition — both of which are **fully engineerable synthetically**. The "natural is superior" claim does not survive scrutiny.

---

## Alternatives (making killing pointless)

- A synthetic version of identical composition has existed since **1904**
- Multiple clean, published syntheses exist in the scientific literature
- In **2023, Conagen** announced the first commercial **fermentation-based** Tyrian purple: engineered microbes produce the molecule at scale, with no snails — described as "democratizing a color once reserved for royalty"
- The molecule is chemically simple and cheap to make; what made natural purple historically expensive was always the snails and labor, never the chemistry
